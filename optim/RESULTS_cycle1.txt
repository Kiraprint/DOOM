======================================================================================

1. THE DEFECT (verified against the installed library)
------------------------------------------------------
sample_factory 2.1.1 constructs its optimizer from a literal dict and passes it
only three kwargs:

    learner.py:228   optimizer_cls = dict(adam=torch.optim.Adam, lamb=Lamb)
    learner.py:~243  self.optimizer = optimizer_cls(params, lr=..., betas=..., eps=...)

cfg.weight_decay is never read anywhere in sample_factory outside Lamb's own
constructor default. Consequences for the campaign:

  * 249 recorded run configs: 0 used adamw, 15 used lamb (which then ran with
    Lamb's default weight_decay=1e-4, NOT the sampled value), the rest adam with
    a recorded weight_decay between 1.1e-5 and 0.095 that reached nothing.
    This includes the released gru_250m_baseline (weight_decay 0.00196).
  * the HPO searched weight_decay loguniform 1e-5..1e-1 and sampled 72 distinct
    values across 86 trials; none of them changed a single update.
  * --optimizer choices in sample_factory are adam|lamb, so the second part of
    the dimension was equally decorative.

CORRECTION to an earlier claim in this work (commit 00fc224's message said "39 of
44 such HPO trials died in 5 s [from argparse]"): that is WRONG. Reading
hpo_study.db's trial_user_attributes rather than inferring from durations:

     35 of 41 FAIL trials with an error message failed on
        "unable to allocate shared memory(shm) for file </torch_*>: Success (0)"
        4 on "torch._C._CudaDeviceProperties object has no attribute 'total_mem'"
     7 FAIL trials are at the adam index and 34 at the adamw index, so the
        optimizer is not the discriminator;
     22 adamw-index trials COMPLETED, with durations up to 4159 s.

So the dimension was inert, not fatal. Additionally the study's code state is not
recoverable: the recorded study has three optimizer categories (including adamw),
while the cluster's current hpo/search_space.py restricts the dimension to
["adam","lamb"] and hpo/evaluate.py hard-codes "--optimizer", "adam".

What IS verified and unchanged: no recorded run had its weight_decay reach an
optimizer, and adamw was never an available SF choice. Any claim that the HPO's
"GRU+HPO" gain came from tuning weight_decay or optimizer is unsupported; the
effective search space was lr (72 distinct values), exploration_loss_coeff (72),
rnn_num_layers, and the mamba_*/transformer_*/perceiver_* architecture knobs.

3. WHY COUPLED L2 HURTS: A MECHANISM, NOT A TUNING EFFECT
---------------------------------------------------------
torch.optim.Adam(weight_decay=wd) adds wd*theta to the gradient; Adam then
divides by sqrt(v)+eps. For any parameter whose own gradient is small relative to
the decay term, the normalised step becomes approx +-lr regardless of how small
wd is -- the decay IS the update. Measured at this campaign's real lr and eps
(200 updates on a 64x64 tensor, deliberately tiny gradients):

  adam, coupled L2, wd 1.96e-3 : ||theta|| 3.179 -> 1.202  (-62%)
  adamw, decoupled, wd 1.96e-3 : ||theta|| 3.129 -> 3.159  (+1%)

A 50M-frame run makes ~12.2k updates (50M/4096), so the coupled arm sits under
continuous shrinkage for the whole run. This is why the HPO's weight_decay range
could never have been useful through the adam path, independently of the fact
that it was never plumbed.

4. WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED
---------------------------------
ESTABLISHED: coupled L2 under Adam at eps 1e-6 costs -5.49 +- 0.48 vs no decay
  (paired, 3 seeds), with a mechanism demonstrated at the same lr/eps.
ESTABLISHED: decoupled decay at the same wd is not equivalent to coupled L2, and
  differs from no decay by +1.04 +- 2.21 -- i.e. NOT a demonstrated improvement.
NOT ESTABLISHED: that AdamW helps on this task; n=3 and the interval includes 0.
WITHDRAWN: the muon cell. Two defects in optimizers_ext.Muon made it test
  something else: (a) the AdamW fallback group inherited the Muon lr, so
  embeddings/biases/norms were driven at 0.02 (50x the tuned 4.05e-4) and
  (b) weight_decay never reached the orthogonalised group, so the arm labelled
  "+ wd" decayed only the fallback. Both fixed (fallback_lr, decoupled decay in
  _step_muon, --muon_lr -> cfg.muon_lr) and unit-tested.
